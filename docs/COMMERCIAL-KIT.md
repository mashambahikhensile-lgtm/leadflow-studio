## Initial offer: Lead Capture & Follow-Up Starter

**Outcome:** a small service business can capture website enquiries into a structured record and manage a simple follow-up status, with one agreed handoff to an existing system where feasible.

**Ideal buyer:** a small home-service or local professional business (e.g., renovation, cleaning, electrical, maintenance) with a working website and a clear enquiry process, but no reliable way to route form submissions.

**US$450 fixed-price target scope (proposed, not validated):**
- One responsive enquiry form with up to 8 agreed fields.
- Server-side validation and useful visitor-facing error states.
- Persistent lead storage in one agreed managed database or existing CRM (chosen after discovery).
- Duplicate handling based on an agreed key/window.
- One destination integration (one webhook/API endpoint or one supported CRM, not several).
- Basic admin list/search and status update if the selected system does not already provide it.
- Test checklist, deployment, documentation, and one handover walkthrough.

**Excluded:** paid third-party fees; full CRM migration; multi-branch routing; multi-step SMS/email campaigns; telephony; AI qualification; bespoke analytics; multiple integrations; complex identity/access management; copywriting; redesign of the whole website; ongoing monitoring. These need separate scope and pricing.

**Timeline:** 5–8 business days after access, requirements, destination account, and approval are provided. This estimate assumes one simple integration and timely client feedback; it is not a guarantee.

**Client responsibilities:** provide access to the website/repository and destination account, confirm field definitions and consent language, nominate a workflow owner, provide test cases, and approve deployment.

**Acceptance criteria:** agreed fields submit on desktop/mobile; invalid input is rejected; valid lead is saved once; agreed destination receives the correct mapped fields; errors are visible/logged; admin access is restricted; test checklist passes; source and handover notes are delivered.

**Revision policy:** one consolidated revision round for in-scope visual/field adjustments. New destinations, fields that alter the data model, or new workflow branches require a change order.

**Maintenance:** optional monthly support from US$50–$100 for a capped 1–2 hours of monitoring/small fixes, only if the hosting and third-party systems support it. Not included in build price.

**Price rationale:** US$450 corresponds to a tightly bounded project, not a claim about market average. Before quoting it to a real buyer, scope integration complexity and test it end-to-end. A first paid engagement can be a smaller, clearly bounded $100–$250 pilot if that is what the evidence and buyer risk justify; do not promise production-grade multi-system automation without experience.

Apply only if at least 7/10 are true:
- The outcome is clearly described.
- At least 70% of the required work is already within tested skills.
- The portfolio proves the closest matching capability.
- The client has a clear budget and deliverables.

- Scope fits a short milestone.
- No unproven regulated/security-critical system is involved.
- Proposal count is not already extreme unless the fit is unusually strong.

## Low-cost validation experiment

**Hypothesis:** small service businesses with website enquiries will pay for a narrowly scoped lead-intake and one-destination workflow when it reduces manual handling and has clear ownership.

Over 14 days:
1. Identify 15 relevant Upwork jobs or public buyer discussions with explicit form/lead/workflow pain. Record budget, requested stack, scope, and buyer proof; do not message outside platform in violation of rules.
2. Interview or ask 5–8 potential buyers/agency partners concise questions about their current lead flow, failure points, existing tools, and what they would pay to fix first. Do not pitch a fake case study.
3. Show the working demo and ask which step is useful, what is missing, and what would prevent adoption.
4. Track concrete signals: replies, requests for a quote, willingness to share a workflow diagram, paid pilot interest, and repeated objections.

**Proposed decision thresholds:** continue if at least 3 of 8 conversations independently describe the same problem and at least 2 ask for a quote or next step; refine if people recognise the pain but reject the proposed workflow or price; reconsider the niche if fewer than 2 of 8 see the problem as important after a clear demo. These are operating targets for a small experiment, not industry benchmarks. A completed portfolio project alone is not validation.
